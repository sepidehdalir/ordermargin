Create a quote

Add a client in Library if needed. Choose Create an order, enter a title and customer-facing scope, select the client and choose currency before adding costs. Saved order currency is fixed; the app does not convert exchange rates. Select a status and optional due date.

Enter real costs

Add materials using purchase cost, package quantity and the quantity consumed. Compatible units convert automatically; weight and volume cannot mix, and a pack is not automatically a number of pieces. Labor uses minutes and an hourly rate. Add packaging, delivery, setup, subcontracting or other costs. An order keeps a copy of library prices, so later changes do not rewrite old work.

Understand the price

Overhead is a percentage of direct costs. Markup increases total entered cost; target margin calculates cost divided by one minus the requested margin. A 50% markup and 50% margin produce different prices. Target-margin prices round upward to the currency’s smallest unit. Estimates exclude any unentered costs. A custom price may show an estimated loss.

Tax, deposit and payments

Enter an optional applicable customer tax percentage; the app does not determine tax rules. Tax is added to the selling price and excluded from estimated profit. Request a percentage deposit or a fixed amount up to the customer total. Record payments you have actually received in Payments. Overpayments appear as a credit. Editing or removing a payment record changes the recorded balance and does not move or refund money.

Preview and share

Save the order, then choose Preview & share PDF. Inspect every page and use Share to save to Files or another destination. Customer quotes include scope, selling price, tax, deposit, payments, remaining balance and terms. Internal costs, rates and estimated profit are excluded. Pro enables business branding and Atelier styling; check Business & defaults before applying updated business details to an existing order.

Keep your work safe

Export library backup in Settings and save the .ordermargin file outside the app. Import in Settings or open a backup file with OrderMargin. Review the import before confirming: records receive new identities and quote numbers; existing work is preserved. Importing the same backup twice creates two copies. Choose Import orders as archived to access a larger backup on Free. Choose explicitly whether to import business defaults. Imported completed work and payments count in reports as new records.

Archive and Trash

Completed, cancelled, archived and trashed orders do not use the active-order allowance. Trash is recoverable through the Orders filter and excluded from reports. Archived orders remain in historical reports. Restoring an active order checks your allowance. No automatic purge occurs. Removing the app removes its local library; keep external backups.

Monthly summaries

Sales and estimated profit use completed orders’ completion dates and exclude customer tax. Cash receipts use payment dates and may include deposits on unfinished work. Reports separate currencies. Free shows the latest month for the selected currency; Pro adds all months, cost/cash detail and internal CSV export. CSV and backups contain private information and are not customer quotes.

Annual and Lifetime Pro

Free includes three active orders, basic calculations, manual payments, Classic PDFs, materials and safety backups. Pro adds unlimited active orders, order/labor templates, branding, advanced reports, CSV and premium styling, subject to local storage and safe file limits. The app displays Apple’s actual localized prices. Annual Pro auto-renews unless cancelled in Apple subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not cancel an existing annual subscription. Use Restore Purchases to restore access; it does not restore order data. Saved work remains readable, editable and exportable if annual access ends.

Reminders and failures

Enable reminders only on orders with a due date. The nearest 50 upcoming active orders are scheduled locally; past dates are not scheduled. iOS permission and Focus settings apply. Reopen the app after restoring data or changing time zones. If a save fails, the previous library remains intact. Unreadable files are preserved and reported instead of silently reset. A valid backup can replace an unreadable library after an explicit recovery confirmation; the original file is retained separately. Invalid or unsupported backups are rejected.
