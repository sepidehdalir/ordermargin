PRIVACY POLICY

Your business.
On your device.

Effective October 22, 2026 · OrderMargin 1.0 · Developer: Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir

What the developer collects

The developer does not collect data through OrderMargin. The app has no developer-operated server, account system, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. Orders, clients, entered costs, manual payment records, business details and selected logos are processed and stored locally.

Your logo and reminders

Apple’s photo picker lets you choose a logo image. Only that selection is copied and resized locally; the developer does not receive the image or obtain access to your photo library. Optional due reminders use local notifications after you grant permission. The quote number and order title may appear on the lock screen according to your iOS settings. There is no remote push service.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple handles Annual Pro subscriptions, Lifetime Pro purchases and purchase restoration. The app checks verified entitlements locally. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple may process purchase, device backup and diagnostic information under its own policies. iOS device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings. OrderMargin does not provide its own cloud synchronization.

Files you choose to share

A PDF, CSV or backup is shared only through a destination you select, such as Files, AirDrop or another app. Customer PDFs exclude internal cost and estimated profit details. Internal CSV and backups can include private business and client information; choose recipients carefully. Destination services process shared files under their own policies.

Retention and control

You can edit your local records, archive work, move orders to recoverable Trash, and export a portable backup. Archived and trashed records remain in the app and backups. Removing OrderMargin removes its local app data, subject to Apple’s device backup behavior. Copies previously exported or shared remain wherever you saved or sent them. Keep a backup outside the app before removing it.

Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send. Avoid client information, payment details and credentials. This source-free website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web-server information under GitHub’s policy. The website has no analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by the developer.
